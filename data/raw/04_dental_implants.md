# Dental Implants

## What is a dental implant?
A dental implant is an artificial tooth root, usually made of titanium, that is surgically placed into the jawbone. Once it fuses with the bone (osseointegration), a crown, bridge, or denture is attached on top to replace one or more missing teeth. Implants are considered the gold standard for tooth replacement because they look, feel, and function like natural teeth.

## Who is a good candidate for implants?
- Adults with one or more missing teeth
- People with sufficient jawbone density to support the implant
- Non-smokers or those willing to quit (smoking significantly reduces success rates)
- People with well-controlled general health conditions
- Those with good oral hygiene

People with uncontrolled diabetes, active gum disease, or insufficient bone may need preparatory treatment before implants are possible.

## What does the implant process look like in Sweden?
The process typically takes 3–9 months from start to finish:
1. **Consultation and X-rays/3D scan**: Bone volume and health assessed
2. **Bone grafting (if needed)**: If bone volume is insufficient, bone graft is performed and healed before implant placement (adds several months)
3. **Implant placement**: Titanium screw placed surgically into the bone under local anesthesia. Healing period of 3–6 months follows (osseointegration)
4. **Abutment placement**: A connector piece is attached to the implant
5. **Crown placement**: A custom-made crown is fitted on top

## How much do implants cost in Sweden?
Dental implants are expensive. A single implant including the crown typically costs between 15,000–30,000 SEK at private clinics. Folktandvården also offers implants in some regions, usually at lower cost.

Implants that replace missing teeth are covered by högkostnadsskyddet, so for an expensive implant treatment you pay the first 3,000 SEK, then 50% of the cost up to 15,000 SEK, and 15% of the cost above that, calculated on the reference price. For people born in 1959 or earlier, implants in the ten front teeth of each jaw are covered by tiotandvård, meaning the patient pays 10% of the reference price. The clinic must give you a written cost estimate before treatment starts.

## Are implants painful?
The surgery is performed under local anesthesia and is not painful during the procedure. Swelling, bruising, and mild discomfort for 3–5 days afterward is expected and managed with prescribed or over-the-counter pain relief.

## How long do implants last?
With proper care, implants can last 20–30 years or even a lifetime. The crown on top may need replacement after 10–15 years due to normal wear.

## What is the success rate of implants?
Dental implants have a success rate of approximately 95–98% over 10 years when placed in healthy patients with adequate bone. Smoking, uncontrolled diabetes, and poor oral hygiene reduce success rates significantly.

## How do I care for an implant?
Implants require the same care as natural teeth:
- Brush twice daily
- Clean around the implant with interdental brushes (mellanrumsborstar)
- Attend regular dental checkups — implants can develop peri-implantitis (gum disease around implants) if not maintained

## What is peri-implantitis?
Peri-implantitis is an inflammatory condition affecting the gum and bone around a dental implant, similar to periodontitis around natural teeth. It is caused by bacterial buildup and can lead to bone loss and implant failure if untreated. Regular maintenance and good home care are essential to prevent it.

## Are there alternatives to implants?
- **Fixed bridge (bro)**: An artificial tooth anchored to the adjacent natural teeth. Less expensive than an implant but requires grinding down healthy neighboring teeth.
- **Partial denture (partiell protes)**: A removable option, less stable than an implant or bridge.
- **Full denture (helbro/helprotes)**: For complete tooth loss.
