# Toothache and Dental Emergencies

## What causes a toothache?
A toothache is pain in or around a tooth. Common causes include:
- Tooth decay (cavity) that has reached the nerve
- A cracked or fractured tooth
- An abscess (bacterial infection at the root tip)
- Gum disease causing bone loss around the root
- A loose or damaged filling
- Teeth grinding (bruxism) causing soreness
- Wisdom tooth eruption or impaction
- Referred pain from sinusitis (pain felt in upper back teeth)

## When is a toothache a dental emergency?
Seek dental care urgently if you experience:
- Severe, throbbing pain that does not respond to pain medication
- Swelling of the face, jaw, or neck (can indicate a spreading infection — go to an emergency room)
- Fever alongside toothache
- Difficulty breathing or swallowing (go to an emergency room immediately)
- A knocked-out permanent tooth

## My face or cheek is swollen, or I have a fever with a toothache. What should I do?
Swelling in the face, cheek, jaw, or neck together with a toothache can mean that a dental infection is spreading. This needs care the same day.
- If you have difficulty breathing or swallowing, or the swelling is spreading quickly toward the eye or neck, go to the nearest emergency room (akutmottagning) immediately or call 112.
- If your face or jaw is swollen, or you have a fever with a toothache, contact a dental clinic for an emergency appointment today. Outside clinic hours, call 1177 Vårdguiden or go to the emergency room.
- Antibiotics alone do not cure a dental infection. The tooth causing it must be treated, usually with a root canal or an extraction.
- While waiting, you can take pain relief, but do not wait to see if the swelling goes away on its own.

## What should I do while waiting to see a dentist?
- Take over-the-counter pain relief (ibuprofen is generally more effective for dental pain than paracetamol)
- Apply a cold compress to the outside of the cheek — 20 minutes on, 20 minutes off
- Avoid very hot, cold, or sweet foods that may worsen pain
- Clove oil (eugenol) applied to the tooth can temporarily relieve pain
- Do not place aspirin directly on the gum — this can burn tissue

## How does emergency dental care work in Sweden?
In Sweden, emergency dental care (akuttandvård) is available through several channels:
- **Folktandvården**: Most Folktandvården clinics have emergency slots for acute pain, particularly during working hours. Call your local clinic directly.
- **Privat akuttandvård**: Many private dental clinics have emergency hours or dedicated acute appointments.
- **1177 Vårdguiden**: Call 1177 at any time for guidance on whether your situation is a dental emergency and to be directed to the appropriate care. They can also help outside of normal clinic hours.
- **Akutmottagning (emergency room)**: For dental infections causing facial swelling, difficulty breathing or swallowing, or high fever — go directly to the nearest emergency room as these can be life-threatening.

## What is a dental abscess?
An abscess is a pocket of pus caused by bacterial infection. Dental abscesses can form at the root tip (periapical abscess) or in the gum next to a tooth (periodontal abscess). Symptoms include severe throbbing pain, swelling, fever, and a bad taste in the mouth from draining pus. Abscesses require prompt treatment — antibiotics alone are not sufficient and the source of infection must be treated (root canal or extraction).

## What should I do if a tooth is knocked out?
- **Baby tooth**: Do not try to replant it. See a dentist to check for injury.
- **Permanent tooth**: This is a time-sensitive emergency.
  1. Pick up the tooth by the crown (the white part), not the root
  2. If dirty, rinse gently with clean water — do not scrub
  3. Try to reinsert it into the socket immediately if possible, and bite down gently on a cloth to hold it
  4. If you cannot reinsert it, keep it moist by placing it in a glass of milk or holding it between your cheek and gum
  5. Get to a dentist within 30 minutes — the sooner, the better

## What if a tooth is cracked or chipped?
- Rinse your mouth with warm water
- Save any pieces if possible
- Apply a cold compress to reduce swelling
- See a dentist as soon as possible — small chips may just need smoothing, but cracks extending to the root may require a crown or extraction

## What are the costs for emergency dental care in Sweden?
Emergency dental visits are covered under the same system as regular dental care — the högkostnadsskydd applies. If you are registered with Folktandvården you will be seen and billed according to the national fee schedule. Emergency visits outside normal hours at private clinics may carry a higher call-out fee, but the core treatment is still subject to the cost protection rules.
