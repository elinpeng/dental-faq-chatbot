# The Swedish Dental Subsidy System

## How does dental care financing work in Sweden?
Dental care in Sweden is not fully publicly funded for adults in the same way as general healthcare. Adults pay a large part of dental costs themselves, but the state provides support to keep costs manageable:
1. **Tandvårdsbidraget** — an annual general dental care allowance
2. **Högkostnadsskyddet** — a high-cost protection scheme for larger treatments
3. **Tiotandvård** — a reform from 1 January 2026 for people born in 1959 or earlier (aged 67 and over)

Children and young people receive free dental care until 31 December of the year they turn 19. Adults start paying from the year they turn 20.

## What is tandvårdsbidraget?
Tandvårdsbidraget (the general dental care allowance) is an annual allowance from the state for everyone insured in Sweden from the year they turn 20. It is meant to encourage regular checkups and preventive care.

The amount depends on your age:
- **Ages 20–29**: 600 SEK per year
- **Ages 30–64**: 300 SEK per year
- **Ages 65 and over**: 600 SEK per year

You do not need to apply. When it is time to pay, tell your dental clinic that you want the allowance applied, and it is deducted from your bill. Unused allowance can be saved, so you can use up to two years' worth at once. The allowance can also be used to pay for subscription dental care.

## What is högkostnadsskyddet?
Högkostnadsskyddet (high-cost protection) limits how much you pay for dental treatment over a period of 12 consecutive months. It applies to everyone insured in Sweden from the year they turn 20.

How it works:
- **Up to 3,000 SEK**: You pay the full cost yourself
- **3,000–15,000 SEK**: You pay 50% of the cost in this range
- **Above 15,000 SEK**: You pay 15% of the cost above this level

Example: a treatment costing 20,000 SEK (at reference price) costs you 3,000 + 6,000 + 750 = 9,750 SEK.

If you use tandvårdsbidraget, it is deducted from the total before the high-cost protection is calculated. You can choose to start a new 12-month period early, which can be worthwhile before an extensive and expensive treatment.

## What does högkostnadsskyddet not cover?
- Teeth whitening and other cosmetic dental care
- Dental care within a subscription dental care agreement
- The part of a clinic's price that exceeds the reference price
- Dental care that is part of treating an illness — this is instead charged at the region's regular patient fee, the same as medical care. For people aged 85 and older, this type of dental care is free.

## What is tiotandvård — the reform for people aged 67 and over?
From 1 January 2026, people born in 1959 or earlier are covered by **tiotandvård** (ten-tooth care), a new high-cost protection.

Under tiotandvård you pay only **10% of the reference price** for treatments such as:
- Fillings
- Treatment of disease, pain, and tooth loss
- Crowns, bridges, and implants in the **ten front teeth** of the upper and lower jaw (tooth positions 1–5 on each side, from the front tooth back to and including the first two premolars)

Tiotandvård does **not** cover:
- Examinations
- Preventive care such as cleaning and tartar removal
- X-rays

For treatments covered by tiotandvård, the dentist or dental hygienist is not allowed to charge more than the reference price. Everything not covered by tiotandvård still falls under the regular högkostnadsskydd (pay the first 3,000 SEK, then 50%, then 15% above 15,000 SEK). People in this age group also receive tandvårdsbidraget of 600 SEK per year.

## What does dental care cost for elderly people?
- People aged 67 and over (born 1959 or earlier) are covered by tiotandvård and pay 10% of the reference price for fillings, treatment of pain and disease, and crowns, bridges, and implants in the front ten teeth.
- People aged 65 and over receive 600 SEK per year in tandvårdsbidrag.
- Dental care that is part of treating an illness is free for people aged 85 and older.
- Elderly people living in special housing or receiving home care are entitled to an oral health assessment and can receive necessary dental care at the same cost as medical care. Many regions have mobile dental teams that visit nursing homes.

## What is Folktandvården?
Folktandvården is the publicly owned dental care provider in Sweden, run by each region. It has clinics throughout the country, including in rural areas. Folktandvården treats all age groups, offers emergency dental care, and is the main provider of free dental care for children and young people.

## Can I go to a private dentist instead?
Yes. You can choose any dental clinic. Almost all private clinics are affiliated with the state dental subsidy system and apply the same allowances and high-cost protection. Private clinics set their own prices, which can be higher than Folktandvården's. If a clinic charges more than the reference price, you pay the difference yourself and it does not count toward the high-cost protection.

## What is the reference price?
The reference price (referenspris) is a price set by the state for each dental treatment. The high-cost protection is calculated from the reference price, not from the clinic's own price. Clinics may charge more than the reference price for regular treatments, but not for treatments covered by tiotandvård. Before treatment, your clinic must tell you what it will cost, and for treatments spread over several visits you should receive a written cost estimate.

## What is the special dental care allowance?
The special dental care allowance (särskilt tandvårdsbidrag) is for people with certain diseases or disabilities that increase the risk of dental problems. It is meant for preventive care. Examples include:
- People with dry mouth caused by medication or radiation treatment to the head and neck
- People with certain chronic diseases such as Sjögren's syndrome or Crohn's disease
- People with disabilities that make it hard to maintain oral hygiene

The dentist or dental hygienist decides whether you are eligible, and you may need a doctor's certificate.

## What if I cannot afford dental treatment?
- Ask your dental clinic if you can split the bill into several payments
- Use your tandvårdsbidrag, which can be saved for up to two years
- Consider starting a new high-cost protection period before a large treatment
- Some clinics offer subscription dental care, where you pay a fixed monthly amount that covers your treatments
- Social services in your municipality can sometimes help with dental costs

## Do I have the right to an interpreter at the dentist?
Yes. Tell the clinic you need an interpreter when you book the appointment. There may sometimes be a charge for interpreter services.
