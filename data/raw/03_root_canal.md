# Root Canal Treatment

## What is a root canal?
A root canal is a dental procedure to treat infection or damage inside a tooth. The dentist removes the infected or inflamed pulp (the soft tissue containing nerves and blood vessels), cleans and shapes the root canals, and seals the tooth. Despite its reputation, modern root canal treatment is no more painful than getting a filling — the anesthesia makes the procedure itself comfortable.

## When is a root canal needed?
- Severe or persistent toothache
- Prolonged sensitivity to heat or cold
- Darkening or discoloration of the tooth
- Swelling or tenderness in the surrounding gums
- A pimple or abscess on the gums
- Deep decay that has reached the pulp
- A cracked tooth that exposes the pulp

## What happens during a root canal?
1. X-ray taken to map the root canals and check for infection in the surrounding bone
2. Local anesthesia applied to fully numb the tooth and surrounding area
3. A rubber dam placed around the tooth to keep it dry and free of saliva
4. A small opening drilled into the crown of the tooth
5. Infected or inflamed pulp removed using fine instruments
6. Canals cleaned, shaped, and irrigated with antiseptic solution
7. Canals filled with a rubber-like material called gutta-percha and sealed
8. A crown is usually placed over the tooth at a later appointment to protect it

## Is a root canal painful?
No. The procedure is performed under local anesthesia. The tooth is fully numbed before any work begins. Some mild soreness and sensitivity for a few days afterward is normal and manageable with over-the-counter pain relief such as ibuprofen or paracetamol.

## How long does a root canal take in Sweden?
Most root canals are completed in 1–2 appointments of 60–90 minutes each. More complex cases involving molars with multiple canals may require additional visits.

## How much does a root canal cost in Sweden?
Root canal treatment at Folktandvården typically costs between 2,000–6,000 SEK depending on the tooth and complexity. Private clinics may charge more. The treatment is covered under the högkostnadsskydd system, meaning the state subsidizes a portion of costs above the threshold. Children and young people receive free dental care until 31 December of the year they turn 19.

## What is the success rate?
Root canal treatment has a success rate of approximately 85–95%. A well-treated tooth with a good crown can last a lifetime.

## What happens after a root canal?
- Avoid chewing on the treated side until the crown is placed
- Take pain medication as needed for the first few days
- Return for a follow-up and crown placement, usually within a few weeks
- Maintain normal brushing and flossing

## Is tooth extraction better than a root canal?
In most cases, saving the natural tooth is preferable. Missing teeth can cause neighboring teeth to shift, difficulty chewing, and bone loss in the jaw over time. Implants or bridges are effective replacements but are more expensive and require more treatment steps than a root canal with a crown.
